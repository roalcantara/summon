## Full description (2451 / 4000)

```
The grimoire is restless. Something in it wants out — preferably on your terms.

Summon Demons Safely is a small dark-fantasy ritual game with a very silly heart. Pick a demon from the Restless Grimoire, turn to face the direction he demands, and chant his Latin incantation out loud, syllable by syllable. Hold your focus, seal all four phrases, and a tiny, smug, extremely chibi Goetic demon climbs out of the sigil and into your collection.

THE RITUAL
• Point your phone. Every demon has his own direction. Asmodeus wants the East. Astaroth wants the North. Neither will compromise.
• Chant out loud. The chant tablet lights up as you speak, syllable by syllable, like the world's most ominous karaoke.
• Keep your focus. Wander off-heading or go quiet and the focus clock drains. Run out and a seal cracks. Nothing resets; you just chant that phrase again, slightly more embarrassed.
• Go flawless. Finish without a single crack and the demon's Secret page unlocks.

THE GRIMOIRE
• A demon card for every spirit: rank, House, Calling, legions, and a six-page dossier of abilities, offices, weaknesses and summoner's warnings, all faithfully adapted from the old grimoires and then made considerably less dignified.
• Court Hours: each demon's House holds court at certain real-world times. Summon him while his court is in session and you get extra focus. Bring sunglasses for the Kings of Sol.

THE CATCH
• One summon a day. The summoning circle needs to cool down.
• Bound demons need visiting. Ignore one for two days and he gets restless. Ignore him for three and he slips his seal and escapes. He keeps his lore; you keep your dignity, mostly.

MEET THE FIRST TWO
• Asmodeus, Great King, Lord of Arithmetic & Hidden Wealth. Three heads, one ego, zero chill. Teaches geometry. Will not stop.
• Astaroth, Mighty Duke of Past, Present & Future. Knows your secrets, your future, and that you didn't read the terms of service.

PRIVACY, SERIOUSLY
The microphone is used only during the ritual, only on your device, and only to tell whether you are chanting. No audio is recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and no words are recognised: the app only checks that your vowels and rhythm sound like the chant. The app has no internet access, no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

NEEDS
A microphone, and a compass (magnetometer) to tell which way you're facing. Best played in portrait, somewhere you don't mind chanting Latin out loud.

No demons were harmed in the making of this game. Several were mildly inconvenienced.
```

Why "No" is correct for the microphone: Play does not count data as "collected" when it is
processed only on the device and never sent off it. Audio here is analysed in memory and
thrown away (`PRIVACY_POLICY.md`), and the app has no `INTERNET` permission.
