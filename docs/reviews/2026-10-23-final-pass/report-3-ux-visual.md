# Report 3: UX and visual design

## Summary
The visual and UX design of Summon Demons Safely delivers a highly polished, distinctive dark-fantasy tactical RPG aesthetic with comedic chibi character art. The visual hierarchy is sharp, 60/30/10 dark palette contrast ratios exceed WCAG AAA across all primary surfaces, touch targets meet or exceed 48 dp, ADR-0033's single attention glow rule is strictly enforced, and the Peak-End emotional arc (2.5s multi-sensory climax to celebratory Grimoire fanfare and lore inking) is outstanding. No blocker gaps were found; six should-fix findings cover navigation consistency (EscapeBanner BackHandler, GrimoireHeader back arrow bypassing the card flip), visual alignment (ChantIntro close button inset jump, ChantIntro Rule 3 red wavy line artifact, FeedbackSlot paused amber token mismatch), and header plaque auto-sizing at 2.0× font scale.

## Deliberately different, not a gap
- **Custom dark-only tactical RPG theme (ADR-0001):** Ships a fixed dark color scheme with obsidian navy surfaces (`#0b1326`) and character accents. No dynamic color (Material You wallpaper theming) and no light theme; the dark tactical RPG atmosphere is the core product identity.
- **Orientation locks and letterboxing (ADR-0017 §7):** App declares `android:appCategory="game"` in `AndroidManifest.xml` so Android 17 (API 35+) respects portrait lock for Home/Grimoire and landscape for the Ritual across tablets and foldables without stretching phone layouts. No tablet layout for v1.
- **Linear navigation loop (ADR-0014, ADR-0016):** The application uses a strictly linear game loop (Home → Grimoire → Ritual) managed through Compose `NavHost` without bottom navigation, navigation drawers, or bottom bars.
- **Grimoire primary CTA mid-screen (ADR-0016 / 2026-10-22 review §E):** The Grimoire's "Tap to summon" cue sits mid-screen rather than in the bottom thumb zone because the large chibi demon art directly above it is also a primary tap target, while the lower half is occupied by the tarot demon card.
- **Intro card before every ritual (ADR-0021):** The intro card displays before every ritual by design to orient the player to turn landscape and review the 4 ritual steps; Summon, Back, and ✕ all dismiss the card and initiate the chant.
- **Single attention glow (ADR-0033):** Only one breathing cue glows at any time across the entire game (Home hint chip, unread dossier flip button ⟲, first-time intro Summon button, seeking sigil ring, or chanting line halo), preventing competing visual emphasis.
- **Fixed-size HUD / art slots with capped font scaling (ADR-0033):** Words in fixed art or HUD slots (banner ribbon, seal track numerals, chant feedback line, badges) cap text scaling at 1.3× via `fontScaleCapped()` and shrink to fit, while general body text and rules scroll and scale fully up to 2.0×.
- **No Containment breach or failure game-over screen (PRODUCT.md non-goals):** The game omits punishment screens; running out of focus time cracks only the current seal and reopens the line with a full timer, and missing a chant line allows reciting again without penalty (ADR-0028).

## Needs device check
- **Heading waver vibration and sound feedback in real hands:** Verify physical feedback when the device drifts outside the ±20° tolerance and wavers while the player rotates 360° outdoors or indoors with compass interference.
- **Microphone sensitivity with soft chanting on budget hardware:** Verify whether players chanting at normal conversational volume (rather than loud theatrical projection) reliably trigger syllable voicing across lower-end devices with aggressive noise-suppression mics.
- **Haptic crescendo vibration ramp during the 2.5s summon climax:** Verify the crescendo vibration ramp on physical hardware with eccentric rotating mass (ERM) motors vs linear resonant actuators (LRA) to ensure the climax feels punchy rather than buzzy or delayed.
- **Physical reach of Summon button on ultra-tall landscape phones:** Test physical thumb reach for the Summon button on extra-wide aspect ratios (e.g. 21:9 devices or 6.8"+ screens) when held with two hands in landscape orientation.

## Checked and fine
- **Per-screen visual hierarchy:**
  - **Home:** Eye lands directly on the hopping Grimoire book on the inlaid compass rose dais; court status chip and bound counter establish game context cleanly without visual noise.
  - **Grimoire:** Clear theatrical stage presentation; cut-out chibi demon with accent rim glow anchors the screen; stage chevrons make lateral swiping obvious; tarot card neatly presents identity and stats.
  - **Intro card:** Split stage HUD layout balances atmospheric singer illustration and Summon button on the left with 4 digestible rules on the right.
  - **Chant screen:** Single horizontal axis unifies sigil, vertical seal track, and chant phrase; top HUD row (?/D, FeedbackSlot, ✕) keeps status elevated and non-intrusive; House stage backdrop with darkened radial scrim provides rich atmosphere without sacrificing chant text contrast.
- **Typography system:**
  - Strictly 2 font families: Cinzel (display, headlines, titles, names, numerals) and Inter (body, labels, meta, cues) with tabular figures for numbers.
  - Large-text rules (ADR-0033): Body text scales fully (with scrollable containers); fixed art / HUD slots cap scaling at 1.3× and shrink to fit with `WholeWordAutoSize` and `TextAutoSize.StepBased`; labels share one size per row (`sharedLabelSize`).
- **Color system & Contrast (60/30/10 Rule):**
  - Dark tactical RPG theme: 60% obsidian navy surfaces (`#0b1326`), 30% complementary deep blue containers (`#171f33`, `#222a3d`) with off-white text (`#dae2fd`), 10% vivid accents (gold primary, demon accents, safe green, warning amber).
  - Contrast ratios exceed WCAG AAA for primary text (>12:1) and AA (>4.5:1) for secondary text and icons.
- **Touch target sizing:** Interactive targets across all screens strictly satisfy or exceed the 48×48 dp minimum: `IconButton`s (48 dp), `MenuButton` (48 dp), `StageArrow` (56×75 dp), `DemonCutout` (full stage), `QuoteSlot` (72 dp height), `PrimaryActionButton` (52 dp min height), `SummonButton` (56 dp height), `RoundButton` (36 dp visual inside 48 dp `IconButton` touch target), and `CueChip` (`minimumInteractiveComponentSize()`).
- **State design & Edge cases:**
  - **First run:** Prompt "TAP THE GRIMOIRE" chip with breathing attention halo guides the player into Grimoire; pulsing "TAP TO SUMMON" bubble invites immediate interaction; Summon button glows until first summon.
  - **Empty state:** Shadowy demon silhouette with glowing accent rim, drifting smoke, and pulsing cue.
  - **Cooling state:** Clear countdown to local midnight ("CIRCLE COOLING / NEXT SUMMON IN {H} H") with spring shake feedback on tap.
  - **Restless & Escaped states:** Gamified neglect mechanics with jittering demons, faster book hops, and clear dismissal flows.
  - **Error states:** Actionable `ErrorPanel`s for denied microphone, unavailable microphone, and missing compass sensors with "Allow microphone" and "Open settings" CTAs.
- **Emotional design:**
  - **The Peak:** Multi-sensory 2.5s climax crescendo (dimming, converging embers, rising audio riser, crescendo haptics, flash, rising demon) transitioning seamlessly into the celebration with rotating sunburst, "New Demon Received!" banner, fanfare, and "Awesome!" CTA.
  - **The End:** Newly revealed lore inking animation (`InkLine`) rewards curiosity; flawless summons reveal the coveted Secret lore.
- **Copy & Onboarding:**
  - Witty, comedic Goetic demon lore balances the dark occult RPG atmosphere.
  - Zero-friction onboarding: "Tap the grimoire" → "Tap to summon" → 4-step intro card with visual icons → guided compass turn and chanting. A first-time player understands how to play without tutorials or walls of text.
