# Report 2: Motion

## Summary
The motion design across Summon Demons Safely delivers an expressive, atmospheric dark-fantasy game feel with disciplined system integration. `LocalReduceMotion` is universally plumbed, ADR-0033's "one attention cue at a time" rule is strictly maintained, and physics springs (`DampingRatioMediumBouncy`, `StiffnessMedium`/`StiffnessLow`) drive celebratory peaks (summon tada pop, escape banner drop, speech bubble pop). However, several critical motion gaps exist: 4 infinite animations read `.value` in composition triggering continuous 60–120 fps recomposition; shared element transitions lack explicit bounds and cross-screen navigation uses default raw cross-fades rather than M3 shared axis or container transforms; the Grimoire demon pager uses an unbounded `infiniteRepeatable` sunburst and particle fields behind non-visible pages; and several state transitions (e.g., Grimoire header back/menu, Intro rule checks, and voice meter) rely on linear or abrupt non-spring tweens.

## Deliberately different, not a gap
- **Strict "One Attention Cue at a Time" (ADR-0033):** The app enforces that only one breathing glow exists across the entire screen hierarchy: the Grimoire dossier button (⟲) while unread, Summon until first completion, sigil while seeking/wavering, or chant line halo while chanting. Other interactive elements remain still or use subtle state layers rather than competing pulses.
- **Custom dark-fantasy theatrical timings:** Climax duration (2.5 s), Grimoire opening burst (700 ms), and seal flares (700 ms / 1500 ms) are intentionally longer than standard productivity UI (100–300 ms). These dramatic, narrative pacing decisions align with tactical RPG conventions without sacrificing responsiveness on user actions.
- **Continuous 60s focus clock drain (ADR-0010, ADR-0023):** The focus clock arc drains continuously and accelerates to 3× speed during drift. It does not use stepped countdowns or spring bounces because it represents urgent continuous tension.
- **Word-level karaoke styling without size scaling (ADR-0033 amended):** Syllable and word progress uses color highlights, a 2× line stroke, and a radial glow without per-word font size jumping, avoiding distracting reflows and line breaks mid-chant.

## Needs device check
- **Shared element transition smoothness between Ritual climax and Grimoire stage:** Verify on a physical mid-range device (e.g., Pixel 6a or Galaxy A54) whether `SharedDemonArt` transitions smoothly from the landscape climax into the portrait Grimoire stage without visual stutter during activity orientation reversion (`configChanges`).
- **AGSL HeatShimmer shader frame rate on thermal throttling:** Test API 33+ runtime shader performance in `HeatShimmer.kt` and `HeatDistorted` after 10+ minutes of continuous gameplay to ensure GPU frame rendering remains at 60 fps.
- **Gyro parallax responsiveness on real gyroscope:** Verify that `rememberParallax` smoothing (`0.15f`) and recentering (`0.01f`) feel naturally grounded rather than floaty or jerky on hardware with noisy rotation vector sensors.
- **Chant screen rotation feel:** Confirm whether the transition from portrait to sensor-landscape feels instantaneous and stable when entering the Ritual screen.

## Checked and fine
- **Reduced motion completeness:** `LocalReduceMotion` is integrated across all visual subsystems. When enabled, idle floating bobs stop, jitter is cancelled, dust puff is skipped, sunburst rays stop rotating, tarot card 3D flip cleanly degrades to a 150 ms crossfade, particle fields and heat shimmers cease rendering, and the ritual climax eliminates the bright white flash and scaling pop for a gentle opacity rise.
- **Emotional peak moments (Peak-End rule):** Both the ritual climax (converging sparks, escalating haptics, flash, demon rising) and the Grimoire celebration (rotating sunburst rays, banner drop, pop spring, and "Tada!" fanfare) provide rich, rewarding emotional payoffs for completing a chant.
- **Stable typography during chant auto-sizing:** `WholeWordAutoSize` and fixed-size layout math prevent layout jitter or sudden word wrapping while the player is speaking into the microphone.
- **Haptic coordination with key milestones:** Heading lock, phrase sealing, seal cracking, and climax arrival fire synchronized audio cues and platform haptics, giving tactile weight to physics-based feedback.
- **Efficient frame-rate timing:** `rememberFrameTimeMs()` drives continuous particle fields and shaders through a single frame loop without mutating top-level Compose state, preventing full-screen layout passes.
