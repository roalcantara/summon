# ADR-0031: Five languages, string resources and an English length budget

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-i18n-design.md`
- **Plan:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-20-i18n.md`

## Context

The game was English only: screen text in `strings.xml`, but demon lore, House and Calling
badges and compass point names hard-coded in Kotlin. The owner wants Spanish, Brazilian
Portuguese, Italian and French, without reworking layouts that were tuned to the English text.

## Decision

1. **Languages:** English (source), Spanish (`es`), Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`), Italian
   (`it`), French (`fr`), shipped together. Claude drafts the translations; the owner reviews
   pt-BR; es, it and fr ship as reviewed drafts.
2. **Everything players read is translated:** UI, demon lore, badges and tooltips, compass
   points, the Play Store listing (`docs/store/`) and the privacy policy (English is
   authoritative). Not translated: the app name, demon names, planets (Sol, Venus), debug text,
   and the chant.
3. **The chant is Latin in every language;** the chant check and its calibration are unchanged.
4. **Language choice:** the device language; on Android 13+ also the per-app language in
   system Settings (`generateLocaleConfig`); English for any other language. *Amended
   2026-10-21 for the RC:* play-testers looked for settings, so a gear at the top right of Home and the Grimoire opens a Settings
   sheet with a language picker (Phone language, English, Español, Português (Brasil), Italiano,
   Français; languages named in their own tongue). It works on every Android version
   (`platform/AppLocale.kt`); "Phone language" restores the default behaviour.
5. **Storage:** Android string resources for all text. `strings.xml` holds screen text and the
   compass arrays; `demons.xml` holds demon lore, House tooltips and Calling labels;
   `strings_debug.xml` holds untranslated debug text. `Demon`, `House` and `Calling` carry
   resource ids. PRODUCT.md stays the source of the English demon text; `DemonCatalogTest`
   checks `values/demons.xml` against it.
6. **Length budget:** no translation is longer than its English source, counted in
   characters. `TranslationResourcesTest` enforces it with completeness, placeholder and glyph
   checks. Exceptions sit in `app/src/test/resources/i18n/length-exceptions.txt`, each with an
   entry in `docs/i18n/review.md`, the queue of hard cases (exceptions and translations that
   gave up a joke, a pun or a nuance) that the owner reviews one by one.
7. **Grammar:** counted text uses `<plurals>`; translations never give the player a gender;
   sentences with a compass point read correctly with all eight (no article-dependent phrasing).

## Consequences

- New player text needs all five languages and must pass `TranslationResourcesTest`; lint
  fails the build on a missing translation.
- Debug builds include the pseudo-locales English (XA) and Arabic (XB) for layout checks; pick them
  in the phone's system language list (Developer options on). They are not in the Settings picker:
  on a device English (XA) is unreadable.
- The bundled fonts were Latin-1 subsets without œ, Œ and Ÿ; `tools/gen_fonts.py` rebuilds them
  with Latin Extended-A (same rendering, about 20 KB more per file).
- The device pass found that the legions badge has no spare width: "72 LEGIONES" wrapped, so
  Spanish uses "huestes". Badge labels must fit the English width, not just the character count.
- The English score chip changed from "85 %" to "85%"; French keeps the space.
